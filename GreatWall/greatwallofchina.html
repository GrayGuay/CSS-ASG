Construction

The Great Wall was built over more than 2,000 years, starting from the 7th century BC. Different Chinese dynasties contributed to its construction using stone, bricks, tamped earth, and wood.

  • Length: Over 21,000 km
  • Materials: Stone, bricks, earth, and wood
  • Time taken: Multiple phases across centuries

Military Defense

The Wall was not only a barrier but also a sophisticated military defense system. It included watchtowers, signal fires, fortifications, and garrison stations to protect China’s borders.

  • Watchtowers: Used for observation and signaling
  • Signal Fires: Communicated enemy approach
  • Fortifications: Defensive walls and gates

Tourism

Today, the Great Wall attracts millions of visitors annually. Tourists can walk along restored sections, enjoy scenic views, and learn about its historical significance.

  • Popular sections: Badaling, Mutianyu, Jinshanling
  • Activities: Hiking, guided tours, photography
